# The Verdant Queen

Sylvara is the living world — every leaf, every root, every blade of grass is a part of her. She is the gentlest of the gods, but also the most ancient and enduring. Where others build and destroy, Sylvara simply grows.

## The World Tree

Sylvara's heart is the **Elderwood**, a vast forest that covers an entire continent. At its center stands the **World Tree** — a colossal oak whose roots reach into the underworld and whose branches touch the heavens. Every plant in the world is connected to the World Tree through an invisible network of roots and spores.

Druids serve as Sylvara's voice in the mortal world, tending to the forests and speaking for the creatures that cannot speak for themselves.

## The Cycle of Seasons

Sylvara governs the turning of the seasons. In spring, she awakens and the world blooms. In summer, she flourishes and the world grows strong. In autumn, she prepares for rest and the world harvests. In winter, she sleeps and the world waits.

The **Festival of First Bloom** is her greatest celebration, marking the day when the first flower of spring opens. It is said that on this day, Sylvara walks among mortals in disguise, blessing those who show kindness to nature.

## The Green Pact

Sylvara made a pact with the mortal races: they may use the forest's bounty, but they must give back more than they take. Those who honor the Green Pact find the forest generous — paths appear when needed, food is plentiful, and shelter is always available. Those who violate it find the forest turning against them — paths vanish, berries turn to ash, and roots trip the unwary.

> *"Take only what you need, give more than you take, and the forest will love you as a mother loves her child."*
> — The Green Pact, First Law

## Sacred Sites

The **Grove of Whispers** is a sacred circle of ancient trees where the veil between the mortal world and Sylvara's realm is thinnest. Those who sit in silence within the grove can hear the voices of the trees — ancient wisdom passed down through millennia.
