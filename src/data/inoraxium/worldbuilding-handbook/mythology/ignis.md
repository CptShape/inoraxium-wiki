# The Forge Father

Ignis is the master of fire and metal, the divine smith who forged the world itself. He is the patron of craftsmen, artisans, and all who create with their hands. His hammer strikes echo in every forge, and his breath is the flame that shapes metal.

## The World Forge

Ignis dwells in the **World Forge**, a colossal volcanic mountain at the center of the earth. Here, he works at his great anvil, shaping not only weapons and tools but also the very bones of the world. Mountains are his slag heaps, rivers are his cooling troughs, and gemstones are the sparks from his hammer.

The **Eternal Flame** at the heart of the World Forge is the source of all fire in the world. Every flame, every ember, every spark traces its lineage back to Ignis's forge.

## The Artisan's Gift

Ignis believes that creation is the highest form of worship. Every craftsman who pours their heart into their work sends a prayer to Ignis, whether they know it or not. Blacksmiths, glassblowers, jewelers, and builders are all his priests in their own way.

The greatest artisans are said to receive **Ignis's Touch** — a divine blessing that allows them to create works of impossible beauty and durability. These masterworks are sought after by kings and heroes alike.

## The Covenant of Craft

Ignis made a covenant with the mortal races: they may use the earth's metals and stones, but they must honor the craft. A shoddy weapon, a poorly built bridge, a counterfeit coin — these are insults to Ignis. But a masterwork, created with skill and dedication, is a prayer made manifest.

> *"The hammer does not lie. The metal does not deceive. What you put into the forge is what you get out."*
> — The Artisan's Creed

## Sacred Sites

The **Forge Cathedral** is built inside an active volcano, where the heat is so intense that only the most devoted priests can survive. Its great bellows are powered by the volcano's own breath, and its forges never cool. Pilgrims come to have their weapons blessed in the sacred flames.
