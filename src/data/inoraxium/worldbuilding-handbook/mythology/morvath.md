# The Silent Judge

Morvath is the lord of the dead, the keeper of souls, the final judge of all mortal deeds. He is not cruel — he is simply inevitable. Every soul that leaves the mortal world passes through his domain, and none escape his judgment.

## The Underworld

Morvath rules the **Necropolis**, a vast realm beneath the world where the souls of the dead go to be judged. It is not a place of torment — it is a place of reckoning. The Necropolis is divided into three regions:

- **The Elysian Fields** — where the virtuous rest in peace
- **The Gray Plains** — where the ordinary souls wander in quiet contemplation
- **The Abyssal Depths** — where the wicked face the consequences of their actions

Morvath himself sits upon the **Throne of Scales** at the center of the Necropolis, weighing each soul's deeds on his great balance.

## The Ferryman's Toll

To reach the Necropolis, souls must cross the **River of Echoes** — a dark waterway that reflects every memory of the deceased. The ferryman, **Charon's Shadow**, carries souls across in exchange for a single coin placed on the tongue of the dead. This is why mortals bury their dead with coins — without payment, the soul wanders the riverbank forever.

## The Deathless

Morvath despises the undead — creatures that cheat his judgment by refusing to die. Liches, vampires, and other deathless beings are his eternal enemies, and he sends his **Reapers** to hunt them down and deliver them to their rightful judgment.

> *"Death is not the enemy. Death is the door. Those who refuse to walk through it are the true villains."*
> — Morvath's Edict

## Sacred Sites

The **Hall of Final Rest** is a massive temple built at the entrance to the world's largest cemetery. Its priests tend to the dead, perform last rites, and ensure that every soul is properly prepared for its journey to the Necropolis.
