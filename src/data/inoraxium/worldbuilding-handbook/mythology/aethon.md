# The Sun Sovereign

Aethon is the radiant lord of daylight, the golden flame that banishes shadow from the world. He rides across the sky each dawn in his chariot of living fire, drawn by four celestial stallions whose manes burn with the light of creation itself.

## The Eternal Flame

Aethon's light is said to be the first thing that existed — before the world, before time, before even the other gods. In the beginning, there was only Aethon's fire, and from that fire all other things were forged. The other gods were born from fragments of his original flame, each taking a piece of his power and shaping it into their own domain.

## The Daily Journey

Each morning, Aethon rises from the **Eastern Gates of Dawn**, driving his chariot across the sky. At noon, he stands at the **Zenith of Power**, where his light is strongest and his blessings most potent. At dusk, he descends through the **Western Veil**, and the world holds its breath until he returns.

Priests of Aethon track his journey with great sundials and astronomical instruments, believing that each moment of his passage holds different magical properties.

## The Covenant of Light

Aethon made a sacred covenant with the mortal races: so long as they tend the sacred flames in his temples and honor the dawn, he shall never let his fire go out. Should the last flame be extinguished, the **Eternal Night** would begin — a prophecy that haunts every civilization.

> *"As long as one candle burns in my name, the dawn shall come."*
> — The Covenant of Light, Verse 1

## Sacred Sites

The greatest temple of Aethon is the **Solar Spire**, a massive tower of white marble topped with a perpetual flame that has burned for three thousand years. Pilgrims travel from across the world to witness the **Dawn Ceremony**, when the first rays of sunlight strike the spire's crystal apex and scatter light across the entire valley.
