# The Storm King

Thalorin is the tempest given form, the thunder that shakes mountains and the lightning that splits the sky. He is the most volatile of the gods — quick to anger, quick to forgive, and impossible to predict.

## The Thunder Throne

Thalorin dwells in the **Storm Citadel**, a fortress of living clouds that drifts across the sky, never settling in one place for long. From his throne of crackling lightning, he watches over the world and sends his storms wherever they are needed — or wherever his mood dictates.

His voice is the thunder, and when he speaks, the earth trembles. His eyes are lightning, and when he looks upon the world, the sky splits open.

## The Tempest's Wrath

Thalorin's anger is legendary. When mortals break their oaths, when tyrants oppress the innocent, when the natural order is disrupted — Thalorin sends his storms. Hurricanes, tornadoes, and lightning strikes are all expressions of his divine judgment.

But his storms also bring rain to parched fields, wash away corruption, and clear the air for new growth. He is destruction and renewal in equal measure.

## The Sailor's Patron

Despite his fearsome nature, Thalorin is the patron of sailors and navigators. They pray to him for favorable winds and calm seas, and they fear his wrath more than any other god's. A sailor's oath is considered the most sacred of all promises — breaking one invites Thalorin's personal attention.

> *"The sea gives and the sea takes. Thalorin decides which."*
> — Sailor's Proverb

## Sacred Sites

The **Lightning Cathedral** is built on a cliff overlooking the stormiest sea in the world. Lightning strikes its spire constantly, and the energy is channeled into sacred crystals that glow with captured storm power. Pilgrims come to be blessed by the living lightning.
